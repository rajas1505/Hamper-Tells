Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our handcrafted products, customization & delivery.

Our interactive Hamper Studio lets you select your gift box size, choose your favorite items (artisan chocolates, teddy bear, candles, perfumes, polaroids), pick a signature color-themed ribbon, and compose a personalized message. The pricing updates live in real-time as you customize!

We use rigid shock-absorbent corrugated packaging with eco-friendly shredded paper cushioning. For chocolate bouquets and fresh elements, moisture-retaining seals and temperature-controlled insulation prevent heat damage.

We support dynamic UPI QR code payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Cash on Delivery (COD), and simulated Instant Online Payments for instant receipt and order confirmation.

Orders are handcrafted and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours. Standard courier transit takes 2–4 business days. You can also view live order progress inside your account profile!
